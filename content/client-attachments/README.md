# Client Attachments

This folder is for **client-provided assets** used during site setup and content migration.

## What to place here

- Brand logos (SVG, PNG)
- Product screenshots and gallery images
- Service hero images
- Team headshots for About page
- Legal documents (PDF) for reference when editing policy pages
- Copy decks, brand guidelines, or messaging documents

## Usage

1. Drop files into this directory during development.
2. Upload final assets through the admin panel or Cloudinary integration.
3. Replace `[EDITABLE]` placeholder content in components and pages with approved copy.
4. Do **not** commit sensitive or confidential client documents unless explicitly approved.

## Notes

- Files in this folder are **not** automatically served by the website.
- Reference filenames in your content workflow; production assets should live in Cloudinary or your CMS.
- Remove temporary drafts after assets are migrated to production storage.
